# Feedback & Suggestions

Everyone using the toolkit can help shape it. Any signed-in user — reception staff, practice admins, or clinicians — can suggest new features, propose improvements, report bugs, or flag symptom content that could be better, all without leaving the app.

## Where does my message go?

The first thing the suggestion form asks is **who your message is for**, because different messages go to different people:

| Who is it for? | Use it for | Who receives it |
|----------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **The Signposting Toolkit team** | Feature requests, improvements, and bug reports about the toolkit itself | The national Signposting Toolkit development team |
| **Your practice's toolkit administrators** | New or amended local guidance or symptom content in your practice's toolkit | The admins at your own practice who look after your toolkit content |
| **Your practice's management or clinical team** | Questions about your own practice's policies, procedures, or clinical processes (for example triage or safeguarding arrangements) | **Cannot be sent from this form.** The form explains this and asks you to contact your practice manager or clinical lead directly, in your practice's usual way |

The suggestion form **never** sends anything to your practice's managers, safeguarding leads, or clinicians — so please don't use it for questions meant for them, and never include patient details.

## Submitting a suggestion

There are three ways to open the suggestion form:

- **Navigation panel** — open the menu and choose **Suggest a feature** (near Preferences at the bottom).
- **Help & Documentation panel** — a **Suggest a feature** button sits in the panel footer.
- **Any symptom page** — the existing **Suggest an improvement** action, which pre-selects your practice's toolkit administrators as the audience and links your suggestion to that symptom.

For toolkit feedback, choose a type:

| Type | Use it for |
|------|-----------|
| **Feature request** | Something new you'd like the toolkit to do |
| **Improvement** | Making an existing feature work better |
| **Bug report** | Something that looks broken or behaves unexpectedly |

Feature requests, improvements, and bug reports need a short title plus details. Local guidance suggestions need the symptom or guidance area they're about (filled in automatically when you start from a symptom page). The page you were on is captured automatically so the reviewers have context.

## Tracking your suggestions

Every suggestion you submit appears on your **My suggestions** page — open the navigation menu and choose **My suggestions** (just below "Suggest a feature"), or follow the link in the confirmation message when you submit. Each item shows:

- Its **status** — Pending, Under review, Planned, Done, or Declined
- Any **response from the Signposting team**, with the date it was written

## Knowing when you've had a response

When the Signposting team responds to one of your suggestions:

- A **"… new" badge** appears next to **My suggestions** in the navigation menu, showing how many unread responses you have.
- On the My suggestions page, answered items carry a **"New response" badge** and the response itself is highlighted until you've seen it.

Once you've visited the page, the badges clear — they'll return if the team updates their response.

## Who reviews what

- **Symptom content** suggestions are reviewed by your practice admins in the Signposting settings **Suggestions** tab, as before — the tab shows a badge with the number of pending suggestions, so new ones are easy to spot.
- **Feature requests, improvements, and bug reports** go to the Signposting team, who triage them centrally, set statuses, and respond directly to submitters.
